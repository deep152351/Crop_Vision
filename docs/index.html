Crop disease AI · client-side prototype

CropVision

Message a photo of a crop leaf, get a health read and a treatment plan back — the way you'd text a friend, not fill out a form.

  • 📷Photo-in, read-out.Attach a leaf photo in the chat, no upload forms.
  • 🧮Runs fully in your browser.Pixel-pattern analysis, no server, no account, no cost.
  • 📊Confidence, not just a label.Every read is scored, with a % breakdown of what it saw.
  • 💬Ask it a quick question.Blight, watering, fungicide, prevention — try one.
How this prototype thinks: v1 reads leaf color and lesion coverage directly in the browser (a fast, free, zero-training heuristic) rather than running the trained VGG16 model from the project notebook — that model needs a small hosted backend (e.g. a free Hugging Face Space) to serve it, which is the natural v2 upgrade behind this same chat.
